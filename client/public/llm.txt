### Curriculum
The course is organized into 9 modules covering stock market fundamentals, investing strategies, and wealth-building psychology:

1. The Stock Market - Understanding how markets work and why they matter
2. How to Get Started - Brokerage setup and first investment steps
3. Stocks — Owning a Piece of a Business - Stock fundamentals and research
4. ETFs — The Smart Investor's Shortcut - Diversification and index investing
5. Long Term Investing - Compounding, dollar-cost averaging, portfolio management
6. Reports - Reading financial statements and earnings reports
7. Mindset, Greed & Emotional Discipline - Psychology of investing and risk management
8. Options — What They Are and When to Use Them - Introduction to options trading
9. Building Your Personal Investment Plan - Creating personalized investment strategy
